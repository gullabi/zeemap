# Code of conduct

zeemap follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, criticise ideas and not people, and welcome newcomers. Harassment and
personal attacks are not tolerated.

To report a problem privately, use the repository's private reporting form
([Security → Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/gullabi/zeemap/security/advisories/new)): it reaches only the
maintainers, and conduct reports are welcome there too. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers may
remove comments, close discussions or ban participants who break these rules.
